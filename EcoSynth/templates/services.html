Our Services

At EcoSynth, we provide comprehensive services to promote sustainability and reduce waste. From efficient waste collection to specialized scrap management and community redevelopment, we are dedicated to creating a greener future.

Door-to-Door Collection

We offer convenient waste collection services directly from your home or flat, ensuring eco-friendly disposal and recycling.

Repairing

Our expert team repairs usable items, extending their lifespan and reducing the need for new resources.

Packing Industry Scrap

We manage scrap from the packing industry, transforming waste into valuable materials for reuse.

IT Scrap Collection

Specialized collection and recycling of IT-related scrap, ensuring safe disposal of electronic waste.

Society Redevelopmental Services

We support communities with sustainable redevelopment initiatives, promoting eco-friendly practices and waste management.
